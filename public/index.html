Tamil hymnal web app

Designed & built solo
Vision
Put more than 850 Tamil hymns in every worshipper's pocket and on every church screen, even without internet.
What I made
An installable app with more than 850 songs from three hymnals:
  • Works offline after one visit, and new or corrected songs update themselves.
  • Church projection with a presenter view, clicker support and no PowerPoint needed.
  • Service song lists shared by QR code or WhatsApp, with no accounts or personal data.
  • Tamil and English lyrics with transliteration, and search that forgives spelling.
  • Runs everywhere: Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Chromebook, plus the Microsoft Store.
Impact
A church can run its whole song service from one app: lyrics on the screen without internet or PowerPoint, and the congregation following the same song list on their phones.
